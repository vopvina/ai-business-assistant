# AI Business Assistant

Final project for the Building AI course.

## Summary

AI Business Assistant is an intelligent system designed to help small and medium-sized businesses automate routine tasks. It uses artificial intelligence to analyze incoming requests, classify tasks, set priorities, and support business process management.

## Background

Small and medium-sized businesses often manage many requests and tasks manually. This can lead to delays, mistakes, and inefficient use of employees' time.

The main problems are:
- Manual processing of customer requests.
- Difficulty prioritizing tasks.
- Delays in assigning tasks to employees.
- Lack of automation in everyday business processes.

My motivation is to explore how artificial intelligence can make business processes faster and more efficient.

## How is it used?

The system can be used by business owners, managers, and employees.

The process:
1. A customer or employee submits a request.
2. The AI system analyzes the request.
3. The system determines its category and priority.
4. The request is assigned to the appropriate employee.
5. Managers can monitor tasks and their status.

## Data sources and AI methods

The project may use:
- Historical business requests.
- Task descriptions and categories.
- Task priorities and completion times.
- Employee roles and responsibilities.

Possible AI methods:
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) for understanding requests.
- Text classification for categorizing tasks.
- Machine learning for predicting task priorities.
- Recommendation systems for suggesting suitable employees.

## Challenges

The system has some limitations:
- AI may incorrectly classify unclear requests.
- The quality of predictions depends on the available data.
- Sensitive business information must be protected.
- Human supervision is still necessary for important decisions.

The system is designed to support employees, not completely replace them.

## What next?

In the future, the project could be expanded with:
- A web-based user interface.
- Integration with existing business management systems.
- Automatic report generation.
- More advanced task prioritization.
- Machine learning models trained on real business data.
